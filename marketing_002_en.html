How to use

A diary told through photographs

New entries will appear at the top of the list. If there are no photos, an "x" mark will be displayed.

Created in 3 steps

① Select the date and time
② Take a photo or choose one from your album
③ Write a message

Delete unnecessary diary entries

Swipe left on the diary entry in the list and click Delete to delete it immediately.

Search by date

Looking back on that day with photos and text.

Search by word

You can search the diary entries using keywords, and you can also perform related searches by tagging the diary entries with relevant keywords (#tags), places, people, etc.

Editing function

Clicking on a diary entry in the list will take you to the details screen. If you wish to edit the text, please purchase the editing function. You can also add entries to your favorites, and search for them from the home screen.
